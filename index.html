Privacy Policy for Majalgaon City Last Updated: November 2026 Applies to: Majalgaon City app version 12.0 Prashilkumar P. Khurpe ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Majalgaon City mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data. 1. Information We Collect To provide and improve our local directory services, we may collect the following information: - Personal Profile Information: Full Name, Phone Number, Date of Birth (required, used to verify your identity for account-deletion requests), Gender (Male, Female or Other, asked when you create an account; seen only by administrators and never shown publicly), Email Address (optional), and Password. If you sign in with Google, we instead receive your Google Account's name and email address (verified by Google), a Google account identifier (never a password), and no password is stored. - Contributions and Content: User-generated content including ratings, reviews, and comments on local businesses; and, for accounts specifically authorized to do so, news posts and photos shared with the local community, plus "likes"/reactions on that content. - Rental, Sale and Service Listings: If you post a property listing for rent or for sale, we collect its title, description, monthly rent or asking price, location, GPS map pin, the contact number you give, an available-until date, and photos (currently up to three). A rental or sale listing shows your contact number publicly so that interested people can call or message you. If you post a service update, we collect its title, description, an optional photo, and an until date. The App does not display your name on service updates. - Job Posts: If you post a job vacancy (allowed for owners of a verified business and for accounts we authorise), we collect the job title, description, job type (full time or part time), the optional salary range and location, the contact number you give, the business it is for, and a valid-until date (currently at most 30 days ahead). A job post is shown publicly only after an administrator approves it, together with its contact number and the linked business's name and photo, so that job seekers can contact you. - Ask the Market and Farm Fresh: If you post in Ask the Market (what you are looking for, with an optional title and photo), we store the post and the account that posted it. Owners of verified businesses can mark that their shop has what you asked for, and we record which shop marked it, by whom and when. If we have given your account the farmer role and you post in Farm Fresh, we store the produce, details, price, place, map location, the times you are available, the contact number (and whether it has WhatsApp) you give, and a photo. These posts are shown in the App only for a time we set (currently 24 hours). After that they are no longer shown, but we keep them and their photos for administration and safety. We also record which phones opened each post, when and how many times, and, for Farm Fresh, when someone tapped Call, WhatsApp or the map. Your phone number is not shown on Ask the Market posts; Farm Fresh posts show the contact number you give. - Service Availability Claims: If your account holds the "claim" role for a verified business you own, you may mark that business as offering a specific service update. We record which businesses are marked against which service update. Marked businesses are shown publicly under that service update together with their existing public business rating, and tapping one opens that business's own listing — this does not display your personal name. - Metal Rates: Authorised jewellers or other persons we authorise enter the day's gold and silver rates through accounts with the rater role. These rates are not personal information. - Live Aarti: Persons we authorise, through accounts with the live role, post the link to a YouTube live stream of the Siddheshwar temple aarti and set the daily aarti times. We store only that link, the times, and which account started or ended the live. If you are logged in, we also store whether you have turned live aarti alerts off. When you watch a live aarti in the App, we record that your phone watched that live, when you started and last watched it, roughly how many minutes you watched, and how many times you opened it, linked to your account if you are logged in on that phone, otherwise to the device identifier described below. Administrators use this only to see how many people watch the live aarti. It is not shown to other users or shared with anyone. - Reel Posting Access: An administrator may grant a business owner temporary permission to post reels. The grant date and the number of reels posted under it are recorded. - Business Listing Information: If you register your own business through the App, we collect the business's name, category, address, phone number (and up to two additional numbers), GPS coordinates, description, operating hours, and an optional photo. This information is intended to be shown publicly in the directory once reviewed and approved by an administrator. - Device Identifier: The App uses a device identifier (Android ID). When you register a business or create an account, it limits the number of business listings and accounts that can be registered from a single device, as an anti-abuse measure. It is also how the App Usage Status, the live aarti watch record and the in-app activity records (Section 7) are kept when you are not logged in. When you log in, we also record the device identifier and your phone's model (for example "Samsung SM-A515F") on your account, with when it was first and last used and how many times you logged in on it. Each account can be used on a limited number of phones that we set; to use one more, you can ask us from the login screen, and we may remove a phone from an account. These phone records are kept even after your account is deleted (see Section 8), so that misuse of the App can be traced. They are not shared with anyone, except where the law requires it. The device identifier is not used to track your activity outside the App. - App Version: We record which version of the App your phone runs (for example "11.0"), so we know who is still on an older version and can help with problems that only affect it. It is linked to your account if you are logged in, otherwise to the device identifier, and is not shared with anyone. - App Usage Status: Each time the App is opened on your phone (at most once an hour), it lets us know, using the device identifier and, if you are logged in, your account. We record when the App was last opened, the days on which it was opened and, for a phone without an account, when it was first opened. From the last time it was opened, administrators see whether the App is active (opened in the last 24 hours), inactive, or likely uninstalled (not opened for 30 days or more). This also lets us count people who use the App without an account. We use it only to understand how many people keep using the App. This status only records that the App was opened; what you do inside the App is described in Section 7. It is not shared with anyone. - Location Information: With your explicit permission (via your device's location permission prompt), we access your device's approximate or precise location to show you nearby businesses and calculate distance. You may decline this permission and the App remains fully usable — location access only enables distance-based sorting and is not required for core features. - Connection Information: Basic log data related to synchronization requests with our secure cloud servers. - Login and Security Records: When an attempt is made to log in, we store the IP address it came from, whether it succeeded, the account it relates to (if any), and the time. We use these records only to detect and block repeated failed attempts. They are deleted automatically after 30 days. - Reports: If you report a review, comment, news post, rental or sale listing, service update, job post or other content, we store a reference to that content, your phone number, and the reason you give, so that administrators can review the report. - Website Contact Form: If you send us a message through the contact form on our website, we collect the email address, message type and message you enter, and the IP address it was sent from. We use these only to read and reply to your message; the IP address is used only to limit spam. - Website Blog Views: When you read a blog on our website for at least 15 seconds, we add one to that blog's view count, the same count shown in the App. To avoid counting the same reader again and again, the website stores a random identifier in your browser (it contains nothing about you, and you can remove it by clearing the site's data), and we keep one-way hashes of that identifier and of your IP address (not the address itself) for 6 hours, after which they are deleted automatically. - Website Analytics: Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it -- which pages are viewed, how visitors arrived (for example from a search engine or a shared link), approximate location at city level, and device and browser type. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar identifiers. We do not receive your name or contact details from it and do not use it for advertising. You can block it through your browser's cookie settings or Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. This applies only to the website, not to the App. - Website Courses: Course accounts on our website are created by us or your institute (there is no public sign-up). For each account we store the name and username, a protected (hashed) password, the courses it may open, which lessons you have watched and completed, and the certificates issued to you. Each account works on one device: when you first log in we store a random device code in your browser, keep a one-way hash of it with a short description of the device and browser (for example "Chrome on Android"), and keep your login session with its IP address and browser details until you log out or it expires (at most 7 days). To protect accounts, we record login attempts with the IP address and username for 3 days, and the login page uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that you are a person, under Cloudflare's own privacy policy. While a lesson plays, your name and username are shown over the video to discourage recording. A certificate shows your name, the course, the institute and the date, and anyone who has its certificate ID or verify link can check it on our website. - Push Notification Token: If you grant notification permission, we store a device-specific push-notification token on your account, used solely to deliver in-app content notifications — for example, when a reporter you're subscribed to posts new news, a reel, or an e-paper, or when the Siddheshwar temple live aarti starts. You may decline or later revoke notification permission from your device settings at any time; the App remains fully usable without it, and declining/revoking only disables this notification delivery. 2. How We Use Your Information We use the collected data for various purposes: - To provide, maintain, and secure the App's directory and sync features. - To detect and block repeated failed login attempts, and to review content that users report. - To verify your identity and manage your registered user account, including via Google Sign-In. - To attribute review comments and ratings with your display name to maintain a community-vetted, reliable listing ecosystem. - To review and publish business listings you submit, and to limit business registrations per account/device to prevent abuse. - To publish and manage the rental and sale listings, service updates and job posts you submit, including showing your contact number on them so that interested people can reach you. - To show Ask the Market and Farm Fresh posts for the time we set, to show which shops have what was asked for, and to let people contact farmers. - To limit the number of phones each account can be used on, and to trace misuse of the App. - To show you nearby businesses and approximate distances, only when you grant location permission. - To allow secure offline storage on your device and seamless cloud-sync with our secure servers. - To deliver notifications about new content (news, reels, e-papers) from reporters you're subscribed to, and an alert when the Siddheshwar temple live aarti starts, only if you've granted notification permission; you may unsubscribe from any specific reporter, turn live aarti alerts off with the Live Aarti alerts switch on the Notifications screen, or revoke notification permission entirely, at any time from within the App or your device settings. 3. Sharing and Disclosure We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal identification info with any third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or secondary organizations. Your data is strictly used for the core directory services of Majalgaon City. Business listings you submit (business name, address, phone, hours, etc.) are, by design, published publicly within the App's directory once approved, since that is the purpose of the listing feature — this does not apply to your personal account information (name, phone, password, DOB), which is never made public. Photos, videos, and PDFs attached to news posts, business listings, or your profile (when you choose to add one) are stored with a secure third-party cloud storage service solely to host and display that media within the App — that service does not receive any of your other personal information. Ask the Market posts (without your phone number) and Farm Fresh posts (with the contact number and photo you add) are shown to anyone who opens the Updates tab while they are showing. Service updates are shown without your name. The live aarti video is streamed directly from YouTube and played by YouTube's embedded player (shown through a page on our website). While you watch it, YouTube (Google) may receive information such as your IP address, device and browser details, and viewing activity, and may use cookies, under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not send YouTube your name, phone number, or any other account information. 4. Data Security and Offline Mode The App is built with an offline-first design pattern. Most information is saved securely on your device. When you perform cloud synchronization, your account details and contributed reviews are transmitted securely (SSL/TLS encrypted) to our secure servers. 5.
