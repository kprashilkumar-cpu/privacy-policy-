Privacy Policy for Majalgaon Directory Last Updated: August 2026 Prashilkumar P. Khurpe ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Majalgaon Directory mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data. 1. Information We Collect To provide and improve our local directory services, we may collect the following information: - Personal Profile Information: Full Name, Phone Number, Date of Birth (optional), Email Address (optional), and Password. If you sign in with Google, we instead receive your Google Account's name and email address (verified by Google), and no password is stored. - Contributions and Reviews: User-generated content, ratings, reviews, and comments regarding local businesses and services. - Business Listing Information: If you register your own business through the App, we collect the business's name, category, address, phone number, GPS coordinates, description, and operating hours. This information is intended to be shown publicly in the directory once reviewed and approved by an administrator. - Device Identifier: When you register a business, we record a device identifier (Android ID) solely to limit the number of business listings that can be registered from a single device or account, as an anti-abuse measure. It is not used to track your activity elsewhere. - Location Information: With your explicit permission (via your device's location permission prompt), we access your device's approximate or precise location to show you nearby businesses and calculate distance. You may decline this permission and the App remains fully usable — location access only enables distance-based sorting and is not required for core features. - Connection Information: Basic log data related to synchronization requests with our secure cloud servers. 2. How We Use Your Information We use the collected data for various purposes: - To provide, maintain, and secure the App's directory and sync features. - To verify your identity and manage your registered user account, including via Google Sign-In. - To attribute review comments and ratings with your display name to maintain a community-vetted, reliable listing ecosystem. - To review and publish business listings you submit, and to limit business registrations per account/device to prevent abuse. - To show you nearby businesses and approximate distances, only when you grant location permission. - To allow secure offline storage via local Room databases and seamless cloud-sync to MongoDB Atlas. 3. Sharing and Disclosure We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal identification info with any third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or secondary organizations. Your data is strictly used for the core directory services of Majalgaon Directory. Business listings you submit (business name, address, phone, hours, etc.) are, by design, published publicly within the App's directory once approved, since that is the purpose of the listing feature — this does not apply to your personal account information (name, phone, password, DOB), which is never made public. 4. Data Security and Offline Mode The App is built with an offline-first design pattern. Most information is saved on your device's secure sandbox using SQLite Room databases. When you perform cloud synchronization, your account details and contributed reviews are transmitted securely over SSL/TLS encrypted pipelines to our secure server clusters. 5. Permissions and Access The App requests the following permissions: - INTERNET: required for essential data synchronization. - ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: optional, requested only when you tap the location icon in the App or choose to sort businesses by distance. You may deny this permission and continue using the App normally; declining only disables distance sorting and nearby-business features. No camera, storage, contact list, or microphone permissions are requested or accessed by the App. 6. Children's Privacy Our App does not address or target anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will immediately delete it from our servers. 7. Account Deletion and Data Retention (Google Play Policy Compliant) You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. You can initiate this request from the app's side menu by clicking "Delete My Account" or by visiting our secure external web form. Upon submitting your deletion request, and after we verify it is genuinely from you (via your registered Date of Birth, or registered email for Google-authenticated accounts): - All personal profile information (including your name, phone number, and password) will be permanently and securely deleted from our local SQLite database and secure MongoDB Atlas cloud servers. - All associated login records and account metadata will be purged. - Reviews you submitted will be permanently deleted. - The deletion process is completed within 7 days of submitting and verifying your request. You may similarly request removal of a business you registered, via the App's business management screen, subject to administrator review. 8.
